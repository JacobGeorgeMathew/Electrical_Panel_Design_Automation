## Field coverage expectation

| Categories | Most promising candidate(s) | Good expected coverage | Common gaps that need product-specific PDFs/manual verification |
|---|---|---|---|
| MCCB, MCB, RCCB/RCBO | Schneider, ABB, Siemens, Havells, L&T | Part/order code, series, poles, current, voltage, breaking capacity, standards, mounting, product/datasheet links | Heat loss, exact 3D envelope dimensions, terminal coordinates/conductor limits, clearances, weight, IP. RCCB residual-current sensitivity/type is category-specific, not captured by current target fields. |
| SPD | Schneider, ABB, Siemens, Havells, L&T | Order code, poles, Uc, Up, discharge current, standards, DIN mounting, links | Distinguish In/Imax/Iimp and voltage protection level; legacy `current_rating`/`breaking_capacity` do not represent SPD ratings. Heat loss and terminal coordinates often absent. |
| Busbar / neutral / earth bars | ABB, Schneider, Siemens, L&T and enclosure-system makers | Rated current, system/pole configuration, compatible range, dimensions, order code and product links | Short-time withstand capacity, bar cross-section/material, joint/accessory compatibility, temperature rise, heat loss and terminal coordinates. Many are system-specific. |
| Terminal blocks, DIN rail | ABB, Schneider, Siemens, Phoenix Contact/WAGO (additional candidates), Havells | Order code, current/voltage, poles/ways, conductor range, mounting profile, dimensions, standards, CAD links | Terminal connection coordinates, heat loss and exact stack/configuration. DIN rail is cut-to-length; its length should be treated as configurable. |
| Enclosures | Schneider, ABB, Hager, Rittal, local Indian enclosure manufacturers | Outer/plate dimensions, IP rating, material, product code, accessories, CAD links, weight | Usable layout zones and thermal dissipation are often calculated/configuration-specific rather than provided as one product rating. |

## Recommendation and approval checkpoint

Start by seeking terms clarification or licensed product-data access for **Schneider Electric, ABB, Havells, and L&T**; evaluate Siemens Product Information Hub only under its API-specific terms. The most useful initial families are Schneider/ABB protective devices plus a licensed India-relevant source for enclosure and busbar systems. However, this review did **not** establish an automated-scraping permission or a robots.txt allow result for any candidate. Accordingly, no candidate is yet cleared for a scraper. If the intended approval is to proceed only with manufacturer-authorized APIs/open catalog files, identify which vendors have authorized access or license terms; otherwise the implementation should be limited to permitted manual entry or a source with explicit reuse terms.

No distributor/aggregator was recommended: their terms are not reviewed and no source with clearly permitted automated access was established.
