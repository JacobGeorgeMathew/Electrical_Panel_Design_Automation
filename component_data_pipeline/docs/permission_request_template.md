# Permission request templates

## Email to a manufacturer

**Subject:** Academic request for low-voltage product data access

Dear [Schneider Electric / ABB / L&T / Havells] Product Data or Technical Support Team,

I am an engineering student building an academic prototype of an electrical panel design tool, starting with distribution boards. I am looking for reliable specifications for MCCB, MCB, RCCB/RCBO, SPD, busbar, neutral/earth bar, terminal block, DIN rail, and enclosure products. The fields I need are rated current and voltage, poles, breaking capacity, dimensions, heat loss where available, terminal/connection details, order codes, standards, and datasheet/product URLs.

Could you advise whether you provide an official API, product-data export (for example BMEcat/ETIM, Excel, or another catalog file), or written permission for limited academic use of these data? I will cite the original product pages/documents and will not redistribute your datasheets, CAD files, or the collected catalog data.

Please let me know any access requirements, license terms, attribution format, rate limits, or restrictions that would apply. I can share a short description of the prototype and its intended academic use if helpful.

Thank you,

[Name]
[College / Department]
[Contact email]
